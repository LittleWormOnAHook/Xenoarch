Weather art - sources
=====================

(Scripts/Runtime/Weather/WeatherVisuals.cs, written by Xenoarch > Weather > Create Weather Assets). Original work, no third-party content.
ASH's weather particles use Enviro 3 prefabs (Asset Store EULA) and were not copied.
